[2:30 - 4:00] The Core Differentiator: The Sentinel Safety Workflow (Pay Tab)
(Slide/Demo: Initiating a risky transaction, triggering 'Pause Before Pay')

"Let me demonstrate the crown jewel of our architecture. Let's move to the Pay Tab. Imagine a user receives a panicked phone call from an unknown number, claiming their bank account is frozen and they must transfer funds immediately. The user, acting under duress, types in the amount and hits send.

In any other app, the money is gone. In SenPay, the transaction enters our Risk Evaluation Engine. The AI analyzes the recipient's history, the velocity of the transaction, the time of day, and the contextual metadata. It flags the anomaly instantly.

Instead of a 'Processing' screen, the user is physically intercepted by our 'Pause Before Pay' protocol. The app forces a cognitive break. It visually signals a high-risk alert and refuses to blindly execute the transfer.

From here, the user is offered a lifeline. They can click 'Explain My Risk', where our AI breaks down the exact anatomy of the suspected scam in plain, empathetic language. Or, they can hit the 'Ask Trusted Contact' button, instantly pinging a designated family member to review the transaction before the funds can leave the device. We are re-introducing essential friction into high-risk scenarios to break the psychological spell of the scammer."

[4:00 - 4:45] Proactive Defense: The SafeCheck Sandbox
(Slide/Demo: The SafeCheck Tab and Message Analyzer)

"But what if we can stop the scam before a transaction is even initiated? We built the SafeCheck Tab as a proactive digital sandbox.

If a user receives a suspicious WhatsApp forward, an alarming SMS, or a dubious payment link, they don't have to guess if it's safe. They simply paste it into the SafeCheck Analyzer. Within milliseconds, Sentinel AI cross-references the text against known phishing patterns, urgency-inducing rhetoric, and malicious domains, delivering a definitive verdict. It acts as an omnipresent digital bodyguard."
